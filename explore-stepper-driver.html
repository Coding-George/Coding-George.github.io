Closed-Loop-Ready Stepper Driver

A compact two-board stepper motor driver for NEMA 17 (42 × 42 mm), built around a Trinamic TMC4361A and TMC5160A, controlled over SPI from a Raspberry Pi 4 and designed in EasyEDA Pro.

Back to main portfolio

Two Boards on the Back of the Motor

The TMC4361A S-ramp motion controller drives a TMC5160A gate driver with eight external MOSFETs, giving smooth velocity and positioning moves at up to about 3 A per coil from a 12–24 V supply. Both ICs wouldn't fit on one 42 mm board, so the design is a stacked pair joined by a 16-pin header.

  • Power board: TMC5160A, a dual H-bridge with AO4882 MOSFETs, Kelvin-connected current sensing, and tiered VM filtering sized for hot-plugging over a cable.
  • Logic board: TMC4361A with a 16 MHz oscillator, a 12–24 V to 5 V buck and 3.3 V LDO sequenced through power-good, an ABN encoder input and a level-shifted home-sensor input.

PCB Design in EasyEDA Pro

  • Single-cable interface: power and SPI share one RJ45 cable, with pins on twisted pairs and series termination on the SPI lines.
  • Fewer control lines: enable, reset, freeze and start moved to SPI, cutting the cable to 8 conductors.
  • Stacking-header pinout: power at one end, logic at the other, and a ground next to every fast edge.
  • Datasheet-driven review: crossed SPI data lines, 5 V-to-3.3 V input protection, LDO stability and DC-bias derating of ceramic capacitors.
  • Sourcing: LCSC/JLCPCB where possible, with RS Components and Farnell as UK fallbacks.

Status and Next Steps
